1. Introduction & How the System Works

Dorm Manager is a web-based dormitory billing and management system. It automates the monthly billing cycle — from entering meter readings to generating invoices, collecting payments online, and sending email reminders — so that the dorm office spends less time on manual paperwork.

🔄 The Monthly Billing Cycle at a Glance

  1. Staff enter meter readings for all occupied rooms at the end of the month.
  2. Invoices are generated — one per room — combining the room's monthly rent and electricity charge.
  3. Each invoice is automatically split into per-tenant shares. A room with 4 tenants at ₱10,000/month gives each tenant a ₱2,500 share.
  4. Tenants receive a due-date reminder email on the due date with a PayMongo payment link.
  5. Tenants pay online (GCash, Maya, Card, QR Ph) or in person (staff records it).
  6. The system automatically updates invoice statuses and sends receipts. Overdue alerts are sent to the owner.
💡 Key concept: Rent is always calculated per room, then split equally among tenants. Staff never enter a per-tenant amount manually — the system divides it automatically based on how many tenants share a room.

2. User Roles at a Glance

3. Logging In & Navigation

How to Log In

  1. Open your browser and go to the system URL.
  2. Enter your email address and password.
  3. Click Sign in. The system automatically takes you to the correct page for your role.
💡 Forgot your password? Click "Forgot password?" on the login page, enter your email, and a reset link will be emailed to you. The link is valid for 60 minutes.

The left sidebar contains all navigation links. Your name and role appear at the bottom-left. Click the → sign-out icon next to your name to log out.

4.2 Rooms

Rooms are the foundation of the billing system. Every invoice is tied to a room.

How Room Rate Works

The Monthly Rate you set on a room is the full room cost — not per-person. When an invoice is generated, this amount is split equally among all tenants in the room. For example:

💰 Rent Splitting Example

Room 301 — Monthly rate: ₱10,000 — 4 tenants

→ Each tenant's share: ₱10,000 ÷ 4 = ₱2,500/month

Room 205 — Monthly rate: ₱9,000 — 2 tenants

→ Each tenant's share: ₱9,000 ÷ 2 = ₱4,500/month

Adding a Room

  1. Go to Rooms → click + Add room.
  2. Enter the room number (e.g. "301"), floor, capacity (max number of beds), and monthly rate.
  3. Optionally enter the meter number for electricity tracking.
  4. Set the initial status (Vacant / Occupied / Maintenance).
  5. Click Create room.
⚠️ Changing a room's monthly rate only affects future invoices. Existing invoices already generated are not changed.

4.3 Registering Tenants

🔐 How Tenant Account Creation Works

Tenants never self-register. The dorm office creates their account, which sets a random internal password. An activation email is immediately sent to the tenant with a "Set My Password" link. The tenant clicks this link and chooses their own password — staff never see or handle the tenant's password.

Registering a New Tenant

  1. Go to Tenants → click + Register tenant.
  2. Fill in the Account section: full name, email, phone.
  3. Fill in the Personal info section: birthdate, ID type (dropdown: National ID, Passport, Student ID, Driver's License, PRC ID, UMID), ID number.
  4. Fill in the Emergency contact: name and phone.
  5. Click Register tenant.

The system immediately sends an activation email to the email address you entered. The success message confirms whether the email was sent or if there was a mail configuration issue.

💡 After registering a tenant, the next step is to create a contract to assign them to a room. Without a contract, no invoices will be generated for them.

4.4 Contracts & Rent Splitting

📄 What is a Contract?

A contract links one or more tenants to a room for a specific period. It defines the due day for rent payment and the deposit amount. The monthly rent is not entered manually on the contract — it is automatically taken from the room's monthly rate and divided equally by the number of tenants assigned.

Creating a Contract

  1. Go to Contracts → click + New contract.
  2. Select a vacant room from the dropdown. The room's rate and capacity are shown.
  3. Select one or more tenants from the tenant list. Hold Ctrl / Cmd to select multiple. The live rent preview updates as you select — showing exactly what each tenant will pay per month.
  4. Set the start and end dates of the lease.
  5. Set the due day (day of month rent is due, e.g. 5 = 5th of each month).
  6. Enter the deposit required amount.
  7. Click Create contract.
⚠️ You cannot assign more tenants than the room's bed capacity allows. The system will reject it.

How the Rent Split Works Internally

When an invoice is generated each month, the system looks up the active contract, counts the number of tenants, and creates a TenantPayment record for each one with their equal share. The invoice total equals the full room rent + electricity. Each tenant's PayMongo payment link is for their share only.

4.5 Meter Readings & Electricity Billing

⚡ How Electricity Billing Works

The system does not automatically read meters — staff must enter each room's kWh reading each month. The electricity charge is calculated as:

Electricity charge = kWh used × current rate (₱/kWh)

This charge is added to the room's monthly rent when invoices are generated. The electricity rate is set by the owner in Rates & Settings and is versioned — past invoices keep the rate that was in effect when they were generated.

Entering Meter Readings

  1. Go to Meter Readings.
  2. Select the billing month using the month picker.
  3. For each occupied room, enter the current kWh reading (what the meter shows today).
  4. The Used column automatically calculates: Current − Previous reading.
  5. Click Save all when done.

The progress bar at the top shows how many rooms have been recorded. All rooms should be recorded before generating invoices — rooms without a reading will have ₱0 electricity on their invoice.

💡 The "Previous" column is pre-filled from last month's current reading. You only need to type the new current reading — the system handles the math.
⚠️ Once an invoice is generated for a month, changing the meter reading for that month will NOT automatically update the invoice. Void the invoice and regenerate it if a correction is needed.

4.6 Invoices

Generating Invoices

  1. Go to Invoices.
  2. Click the month dropdown to select the billing period. Each month shows an icon:
    • ✓ green — invoices generated for all rooms
    • – yellow — partially generated (some rooms missing)
    • ✕ red — no invoices yet for this month
  3. Click Generate invoices and confirm. The system creates one invoice per occupied room, including electricity if meter readings exist.

Invoice Statuses Explained

StatusWhen it applies
PendingInvoice generated, due date has not passed. All tenant shares unpaid.
Partially PaidAt least one tenant has paid their share, but not all.
LateDue date passed (within grace period). All unpaid tenants are flagged late.
OverdueGrace period ended. Owner receives a one-time alert email.
PaidAll tenants have paid their full share.

Filtering Invoices

Use the search box to find by room number or invoice number. Use the status dropdown to filter — the counts in parentheses show how many invoices are in each state for the selected month.

Viewing an Invoice

Click any invoice number to see the full breakdown: room total, per-tenant shares, which tenants paid, which are late/overdue, and all payment transactions recorded against it.

Cancelling/Voiding Invoices Owner only

  1. Click the Manage / Cancel link (gear icon) on the Invoices page.
  2. To void one invoice: click Cancel on that row and confirm.
  3. To void all unpaid invoices for the month: click Cancel all unpaid.
⚠️ Voiding an invoice cannot be undone. Paid invoices are never affected by bulk cancellation.

4.7 Recording Payments

How Payments Are Tracked

The system tracks two types of payments:

  • Online payments (PayMongo) — recorded automatically when a tenant pays through the PayMongo checkout. No manual entry needed.
  • Cash / in-person payments — must be recorded manually by staff on the Payments page.

Recording a Cash Payment

  1. Go to Payments. On the right panel, you will see the "Record cash payment" form.
  2. Select an invoice from the dropdown. Only invoices with pending/late/overdue status appear. Each entry shows the room number and how many unpaid tenants remain.
  3. After selecting the invoice, a list of tenants appears. Select which tenant is paying. Their remaining balance is pre-filled in the Amount field.
  4. Adjust the amount if it is a partial payment.
  5. Select the payment method (Cash, Bank Transfer, GCash, Maya, Card, QR Ph).
  6. Set the date received and optionally a reference number.
  7. Click Save payment.

On save, the system: updates that tenant's share as paid, recomputes the invoice status, and sends a Payment Received email receipt to the tenant.

✅ Per-tenant tracking: Each payment is linked to a specific tenant's share. The payment history always shows which person paid, not just that "a payment was made to the room invoice."

4.8 Reports Owner only

Go to Reports to view financial summaries for any month.

  • Total billed — sum of all room invoices for the period.
  • Collected — total payments received.
  • Monthly income chart — 9-month bar chart of collected amounts.
  • Payment status breakdown — rooms by Paid / Partially Paid / Pending / Late / Overdue.
  • Top arrears — top 5 rooms with the highest outstanding balance.

Exporting

  • Export CSV — downloads a spreadsheet with one row per invoice, suitable for Excel.
  • Export PDF — downloads a formatted HTML report. Open in a browser, then use File → Print → Save as PDF for a final PDF.

4.9 Employees & Roles Owner only

Adding an Employee

  1. Go to Employees & Roles → click + Add employee.
  2. Enter name, email, phone, password (you set it), and role (Employee or Owner).
  3. Click Add employee.

Disabling an Account

Click Disable next to an employee. Their account becomes inactive immediately — they cannot log in. Their history of actions is fully preserved. Click Enable to restore access.

⚠️ You cannot disable the only owner account in the system.

4.10 Rates & Settings Owner only

Setting the Electricity Rate

The electricity rate is stored with an effective date, so the system always uses the correct rate for each billing period — even after you raise the rate later.

  1. Enter the new rate in ₱ per kWh (e.g. 12.50).
  2. Set the effective date (when this rate takes effect).
  3. Click Set new rate.

The rate history table below shows all past rates and who set them.

Billing Rules

SettingWhat it controls

Late starts after (days)How many days after the due date before a tenant's status flips to Late.
Overdue after grace (days)How many days after the due date before status flips to Overdue and the owner gets alerted.

PayMongo Configuration

Three keys must be set in the server's .env file:

  • PAYMONGO_PUBLIC_KEY — from PayMongo dashboard → Developers → API Keys
  • PAYMONGO_SECRET_KEY — same location
  • PAYMONGO_WEBHOOK_SECRET — from PayMongo dashboard → Developers → Webhooks (generated when you register the webhook endpoint)

The Settings page shows the connection status (Connected / Not configured) and the webhook URL that must be registered in the PayMongo dashboard.

The system sends automated emails at six key points. This page shows a log of all emails sent, including the type and whether delivery succeeded.

4.12 Audit Logs Owner only

Every meaningful change in the system is permanently recorded here — who did it, what changed, and when. The log cannot be edited or deleted. Filter by action type, user, or time range (7 / 30 / 90 days).

Examples of logged events: creating/editing rooms, registering tenants, generating invoices, recording payments, changing electricity rates, disabling accounts.

5. Employee Guide Employee

Employees have access to all operational features. The only sections not available to employees are:

  • Reports
  • Rates & Settings
  • Employees & Roles
  • Audit Logs
  • Voiding invoices

All other features — Rooms, Tenants, Contracts, Meter Readings, Invoices, Payments — work identically. Refer to the Owner Guide (Section 4) for step-by-step instructions.

📅 Typical Monthly Workflow for Employees

  1. Enter meter readings for all occupied rooms (Meter Readings page).
  2. Generate invoices for the new billing month (Invoices page).
  3. Register new tenants as leases are signed (Tenants page → + Register tenant).
  4. Create contracts for new tenant-room assignments (Contracts page → + New contract).
  5. Record cash payments as tenants pay in person (Payments page).

6. Tenant Guide Tenant

6.1 Activating Your Account

📬 Your Activation Email

When the dorm office registers you, you will receive an email with the subject "Welcome to Dorm Manager — Set Your Password". This is sent to the email address you gave to the dorm office.

  1. Open the email and click the "Set My Password" button.
  2. On the page that opens, enter your chosen password (minimum 8 characters) and confirm it.
  3. Click the button to save. You will be taken to the login page.
  4. Log in with your email and new password.
⚠️ Activation link expires in 60 minutes. If it has expired, go to the login page and click "Forgot password?" with your email address to receive a fresh link.

6.2 My Bill

After logging in you land on My Bill — your personal billing page for the current month.

🧾 What You See on My Bill

SectionWhat it shows
Your amount dueYour personal share of the room bill. This is the amount you need to pay.
Your share breakdownHow your share is calculated: rent ÷ number of roommates, plus electricity ÷ roommates. The formula is shown (e.g. ₱10,000 ÷ 4 = ₱2,500).
Full room billThe total for your entire room (all tenants combined). Shows how much of the room bill has been collected so far.

Paying Online with PayMongo

  1. If your balance is greater than ₱0, a blue "Pay ₱X with PayMongo" button appears.
  2. Click it. You are redirected to PayMongo's secure payment page.
  3. Choose your payment method: GCash, Maya, Card, or QR Ph.
  4. Complete the payment on PayMongo's page.
  5. Return to Dorm Manager. Your bill updates automatically within seconds — no refresh needed.
💡 Important: You only pay your own share, not the full room bill. If your room has 4 tenants, each person pays ¼ independently.
⚠️ If you paid via GCash/Maya and your bill still shows unpaid after 2 minutes, contact the dorm office with your transaction reference number.

6.3 Payment History

Click Payment History in the top navigation. This shows all your own personal payments — billing month, invoice number, your share amount, how much you paid, and your current status.

6.4 Deposit

Click Deposit to see:

  • Required — the deposit amount in your contract.
  • Collected — how much has been received from you.
  • Deductions — any amounts deducted (e.g. for damages), each with a stated reason.
  • Refundable balance — what you will receive back at move-out after all deductions.

6.5 Contract

Click Contract to view your lease: contract number, room, lease period, monthly rent share, due day, and deposit amount. Use Download PDF to save a copy.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

For Tenants

I didn't receive the activation email.

Check your spam/junk folder first. If it's not there, ask the dorm office. They can check the system logs to confirm whether the email was sent and re-send a password reset link if needed.

My activation link says it's expired.

Go to the login page and click "Forgot password?". Enter your email address and a fresh link will be sent. Links are valid for 60 minutes.

I paid online but my bill still shows unpaid.

Wait 1–2 minutes and refresh the page. PayMongo sends a notification to the system which processes it automatically. If it still shows unpaid after 5 minutes, contact the dorm office with your PayMongo transaction reference number.

Can I see how much my roommates owe?

You can see their payment status (Paid / Pending / Late / Overdue) on your My Bill page, but not their specific amounts.

Why is my electricity charge different each month?

Electricity is based on actual meter readings. The total kWh your room used is multiplied by the current rate, then split equally among tenants. Usage varies every month.

For Staff

What happens if I forget to enter a meter reading before generating invoices?

The invoice will be generated with ₱0 electricity. You should void the invoice, add the meter reading, and regenerate. The owner must void invoices.

Can I record a partial payment for a tenant?

Yes. On the Payments page, select the invoice and the tenant, then enter any amount less than their full share. The tenant's status will remain unpaid until the full share is covered.

Can an employee void an invoice?

No — only the Owner can void invoices.

How do I export payment data to a spreadsheet?

Go to Reports, select the month, and click Export CSV.
